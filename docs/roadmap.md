# Roadmap: appliances, remotes, scene engine and robustness (from 2026-11-03)

Status words: `planned`, `in_progress`, `blocked`, `complete`. Decisions are in `docs/decisions.md`. Every package keeps the existing behaviour and saved layouts working, passes `node --test tests/frontend` and `pytest`, and is checked in the sealed test copy of the dashboard before it is deployed.

## P0 Robustness (no new UI)
- P0-1 complete: a "connection lost" notice that stays after Home Assistant reports the connection back is cleared after 3 s; the connection handlers follow a new connection object. AC: the notice clears when Home Assistant reports connected for 3 s.
- P0-2 complete: a battery that only says low (a binary sensor with device class battery) can be tracked, shown as Low/OK, counted in the batteries tile and watched by the low-battery notification (a state trigger on `on`, next to the percentage trigger). AC: tests in `tests/frontend/notifications.test.mjs`.
- P0-3 complete (audited): unavailable and unknown states read "Offline" (`entVal`) and sliders show no value; no row shows a last value as live.
- P0-3 was: unavailable and unknown states are shown as such, never as the last value (audit of rows and popups).
- P0-4 complete (v218): `input_datetime` is a time row (hour and minute, date optional), not a sensor.
- P0-5 complete (v218): the lights master and the section brightness slider keep "the lights that are on" and gain an option "the whole room" (off by default).
- P0-6 partly: the new profiles use no names at all (D-1); the older wizard's name hints (`kinds.js`) are unchanged and still need a rewrite: mode and role discovery uses device class, unit, domain and options, not English names, and every guess can be changed by hand.

## P1 Devices that need a profile, brand independent
Each is a device kind of the page editor's "Add device" list. Its popup is built from slots, each bound with an entity selector; a slot that is not bound, or whose entity cannot do it, is not shown. Only the platforms' own data tells what a control is (domain, device class, unit, options, supported features).
- P1-1 complete: appliance (oven, washer, dryer, dishwasher and any other): program, options, temperature, duration, start, pause or stop, door, progress, remaining time, remote-start permission, free extra rows. Start is disabled with a reason while a bound remote-start permission is off.
- P1-2 partly (v220: map, room buttons, modes, care values; `vacuum.clean_area` by Home Assistant area is not used yet): vacuum profile for what the `vacuum` domain does not carry: map picture (a camera or image entity, or a picture of the user's own), room buttons or a room select, modes, maintenance values, cleaning history.
- P1-3 complete: intercom and camera profile: live view, receive audio, push to talk where the camera supports it, answer and reject on a call, unlock tied to a chosen lock only.
- P1-4 complete: a light with scenes from another entity (a select) next to its normal control (roles on the existing linked entities).
- P1-5 complete: devices with no area of Home Assistant are listed on the new-devices page and placed in a room by the user (saved with the dashboard layout only).

## P5 The scene engine moves into Home Control (decisions D-12, D-13, D-14)
Order: A, then B, then C. Each is released and checked on the real box (with Hue Plus installed nothing on the dashboard changes) before the next starts. Hue Plus's side is in `hue-plus/docs/roadmap.md` (HP-12).
- P5-A in_progress (built and tested; not yet released or checked on the real box): the engine, the saved scenes and areas, the services (`home_control.scene_start`, `scene_update`, `scene_freeze`, `scene_play`, `scene_stop`, `scene_save`, `scene_delete`, `area_save`, `area_delete`, `locate_light`, `get_library`), the live-scenes sensor and the websocket read are Home Control's, for every light. The provider declaration and services (D-13) are in place, and Hue Plus is the first provider. Existing scenes and areas are imported once from Hue Plus's storage. The dashboard talks only to `home_control.*`. AC: (1) the engine's golden and unit tests moved from Hue Plus pass unchanged apart from import paths; (2) a scene on Hue lights plays with the same commands as before, native effects included, through Hue Plus's service; a scene on other lights plays through `light.turn_on`; (3) without Hue Plus installed a scene on Hue lights plays through `light.turn_on` and is not refused; (4) a saved area finds a light again by `unique_id` when its entity id is gone; (5) the first start imports the scenes and areas of `hue_plus.scenes.*` storage and leaves that file untouched, and a second start imports nothing; (6) no screen of the dashboard looks or behaves differently (checked on the harness and on the real box); (7) `hue_plus.scene_*` is no longer called by the dashboard.
- P5-B complete (released 0.7.0/0.7.1 with Hue Plus 0.9.x; the effect catalogue and the Hue menu verified on the real box 2026-11-07): the effect catalogue (groups, icons, labels) and the Hue Plus menu in Settings leave Home Control and come from Hue Plus's declaration (`home_control.json`, `menu.js`). AC: with Hue Plus installed the effect lists and the Settings menu look and work as before; without it the dashboard shows the effects the light reports and no Hue Plus menu.
- P5-D in_progress (D-17; built and tested on the harness, not released or checked on the real box): Hue's scenes, the Spotify sync, the sync box and the gradient style choice leave Home Control and come from Hue Plus through plugin API hooks. AC: with Hue Plus installed the scene rows and popup, the sync rows and popups and the style button look and work as before (on the harness: same controls, same service calls); `frontend/app/` has no Hue-specific code; without Hue Plus a scene of the Hue integration runs like any scene.
- P5-C planned: Hue Plus drops its scene code and `hue_plus.scene_*` services (HP-12.3); scene engine is deleted (D-12). AC: nothing in either repository refers to scene engine; the box runs without it.

## P7 Connection, start and recovery (2026-11-16, the owner's request)
- P7-1 complete (built and tested on the harness and with node tests; deployed to the real box 2026-11-16 and measured there, not yet checked on the phone; released 0.12.0): the page starts and recovers faster and heals itself. Measured on the real box before the change: a page that lost its connection stayed "offline" about 3.5 seconds after the connection was back (Home Assistant's copy of the state, `hass.connected`, lags the connection's own "ready"), a ping to a dead connection waited two seconds, and the start asked for the registries, the plugins, the saved dashboard and the person's choices one after the other (about three round trips; 498 ms at 150 ms per answer on the harness). AC: the registries, the plugins, the saved dashboard and the choices are asked for together (`registry.test.mjs`, harness trace: seven requests out within 18 ms); a connection that says ready clears the offline state at once and it stays cleared (`offline.test.mjs`, with the old line it fails); a page away a minute or more remakes its connection without the ping, a shorter absence asks it (`reconnect.test.mjs`); a changed entity is fetched by `config/entity_registry/get_entries` instead of loading the whole entity registry, and a missing answer, a failure or a burst of more than 200 entities is a full load (`registry.test.mjs`); a lost `version.json` is asked for again three times and a lost file of the application reloads the page up to three times, then a button (`loader.test.mjs`); a save lost to a dropped connection is kept and made again when it is back, and says so (`storecache.test.mjs`); the copies of D-21 draw the page at once (harness, 150 ms: 17 ms against 492 ms) and nothing is saved over them (`storecache.test.mjs`, `registry.test.mjs`).
- P7-2 planned: while the connection is down a command is refused at once (`callRaw`); holding the latest command per light for a few seconds and sending it when the connection is back would remove the "not connected" taps right after opening the app. Not built: it changes what a tap does, so it needs the owner's decision.
